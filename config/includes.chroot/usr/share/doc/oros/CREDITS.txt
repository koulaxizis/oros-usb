Every Debian package keeps its own licence. The full copyright notice and
licence text of each package is in /usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright on
this system.

Source code: SOURCES.txt (on the stick, and /usr/share/doc/oros/SOURCES.txt
on the running system) names the exact source of every program in this
build. Written offer for the source code: WRITTEN-OFFER.txt.
Licence texts of the orOS parts and Go: /usr/share/doc/oros/.
